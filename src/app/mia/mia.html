Mia: helping modern dating feel a little less awkward.

From decoding messages to planning your next move, bridging the gap between matching and meeting.

Role

Sole UX/UI Designer

Team

Product Manager, Engineers

As the sole designer, I worked closely with product managers and engineers to translate product requirements into a clear, user-focused design direction for Mia.

I owned the end-to-end design process, from defining user flows and interaction patterns to designing the interface, prototyping AI conversations, testing edge cases, and preparing developer handoffs to ensure the experience translated smoothly from design to production.

1. Problem Statement

The interesting UX problem began
after matching.

Users already know each other's interests, lifestyle, personality and things in common. But having that information doesn't mean knowing how to use it in a conversation. "Both love live music" is useful. Knowing how to turn it into a natural message is far more useful.

The Problem

After a match, people freeze. They have shared context but no idea how to use it.

The Problem

Mia, a separate AI layer that suggests replies, openers and date ideas from shared interests.

The principle

Mia → User → Match
The person stays in control.

2. How we achieved this

Mia suggests. The user decides.

Instead of generating and sending messages, MIA is a separate conversational layer users enter on purpose. That gives them a psychologically safer place to ask what they might hesitate to ask their match.

Competitive Analysis: Strengths &
Gaps in existing solutions

I reviewed two major dating apps that provides AI assistance and uncovered key insights:

Hunch

Pros

Instant date planning:

Tagging eSia suggests venues and times immediately so matches move offline fast.

Cons

Strict timers and an aggressive push to meet leave little room for slow-paced texting.

Pros

High-intent ecosystem:

Curated daily drops and chat timers keep users focused on meeting in real life.

Cons

No banter support:

Sia handles logistics, offering zero help to socially anxious users on what to say.

Schmooze

Pros

Organic vibe matching:

Voice chats with Riya and meme-swiping match users on real humor and personality.

Cons

Makes starting a conversation easy, but does nothing to help plan an actual date.

Pros
